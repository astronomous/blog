title: 'Exploring the Jamstack'
description: 'A look at the modern architecture for building websites.'
pubDate: 'Nov 01 2023'
---

The Jamstack (JavaScript, APIs, and Markup) is a modern web development architecture that is changing how we build websites and applications. Instead of relying on a monolithic backend, the Jamstack uses a decoupled approach where the frontend is pre-built into static files and served from a CDN.

This architecture offers several benefits, including better performance, higher security, and lower cost of scaling. By leveraging the power of modern build tools and CDNs, Jamstack sites can deliver content to users faster than ever before.

I'm excited to see how the Jamstack continues to evolve and what new possibilities it will unlock for web developers. It's a powerful architecture that is here to stay.
